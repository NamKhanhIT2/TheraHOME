# therahome-callwindow

Keeps a call visible after you leave the app: the call shrinks into a
floating window over whatever you do next, and tapping it puts you back in
the call. Android's picture-in-picture, which is what every other calling
app uses for this.

**Why not a floating bar drawn over other apps.** That is
`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`, a permission the user has to grant by hand in a
Settings page most people never find, and one Play reviews against its own
policy. PiP needs no permission at all, is drawn and moved by the system,
and survives the app being backgrounded. `app.json` deliberately keeps
`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` in `blockedPermissions`; this module is the reason it
can stay there.

**Why a module of our own.** The whole native surface is three Activity
calls. What it really needs is two Activity *callbacks*, and MainActivity is
generated by `expo prebuild` — so `plugins/withAndroidPictureInPicture.js`
injects the two overrides and they call into `CallWindow`, the object in
this module. Read `CallWindow.kt` first: it explains why
`onUserLeaveHint` exists on Android 8–11 and why it must NOT run on 12 and
up.

**Android only.** iOS has no app-level picture-in-picture for this — an iOS
call stays visible through CallKit, which `src/lib/callkeep.ts` already
drives. `requireOptionalNativeModule` returns null there and every function
in `src/lib/callWindow.ts` becomes a no-op.

Used from `src/lib/callWindow.ts`. Local Expo module: autolinked from
`modules/` with no entry in package.json, so there is nothing to install.
