APP NAME 
-----------------
TheraHOME


SHORT DESCRIPTION 
--------------------------
Guided daily neck & back care at home — 14-day plan with video sessions.

FULL DESCRIPTION 
---------------------------
TheraHOME turns your neck and back care into a simple daily habit. Instead of
guessing what to do, you open the app and follow one short guided session a
day — with video, a clear plan, and a record of how you are feeling over time.

Built as the companion app for TheraHOME devices, it works as your day-to-day
guide whether you are starting fresh or picking your routine back up.

A PLAN THAT UNFOLDS ONE DAY AT A TIME
Your program runs across 14 days, grouped into phases that build on each
other. One day unlocks per calendar day, so there is never a backlog waiting
for you — just today's session. Each day opens with a short video you can play
in the app or cast to your TV.

TRACK HOW YOU ACTUALLY FEEL
Before each session you log your discomfort on a simple 0–10 scale. Those
entries become a progress chart on your home screen, so you can see how the
week went instead of relying on memory. A daily water tracker sits alongside
it for the rest of your routine.

GENTLE REMINDERS, NOT NAGGING
Set a morning and an evening reminder at times that suit you. Turn either off
whenever you like.

A COMMUNITY THAT GETS IT
Share progress, ask questions, and read what other people trying the same
routine have found. Posts, photos, comments and reactions — with reporting,
hiding and blocking available on every post, and a staffed review queue behind
them.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Two chats are one tap away: an AI assistant for quick questions about your
sessions, and the TheraHOME support team for anything it cannot answer. The AI
assistant asks for your consent before its first use and never replaces
professional advice.

YOUR LANGUAGE
Full support for English, Vietnamese and Bahasa Melayu, including the daily
program content — not just the buttons.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time: Profile →
Delete account. No email, no waiting on support. Full details are in our
privacy policy at ad.therahomeai.com/privacy.

IMPORTANT
TheraHOME is a wellness and fitness companion app. It is not a medical device
and does not diagnose, treat or replace care from a qualified professional.
The exercise content follows general physical-activity guidance from the World
Health Organization and is intended as a reference only. Talk to your doctor
before starting any new exercise routine, and stop if something hurts.

Some program content requires a TheraHOME device to unlock. Support:
support@therahomeai.com
